Lecture Format: Each lecture is approximately 80 minutes. Starting from Lecture 3, all students are expected to read the two assigned papers before class.

For each paper, the class time is organized as:
  • 20 min presentation (~15 min for the presentation and ~5 min for the critique TODO:)
  • 10 min discussion (TODO:)

The goal of the course is not only to understand the technical content of each paper, but also to practice critically reading, presenting, and discussing research.

Final Project Discussion:

Paper Reviews: Submit a review for each assigned paper before class through Gradescope. Please follow a CVPR-style review format:

  • Summary
  • Strengths
  • Weaknesses (Major / Minor)
  • Personal Interest (1-5) with justification
    • 1: Low, 2: Somewhat low, 3: Neutral, 4: High, 5: Very high

There is no strict character or page limit for the review.

Paper Presentations:

  • Presentation teams consist of 2-3 students
  • Sign up for a paper and presentation slot using the sign-up sheet
  • Prepare a 20-minute presentation of the assigned paper

Slide Submission Policy: Please submit your slides through the Google Form (link TBA) both before and after your presentation.

  • Pre-presentation submission:
    • Monday presentations: due by Friday EOD
    • Wednesday presentations: due by Sunday EOD
    • The slides do not need to be fully polished, but they should at least clearly show the overall structure and flow of your presentation.
    • You can continue editing your slides after this submission.
    • The teaching staff may provide feedback if needed.
  • Final submission: submit the final version of your slides by the end of your presentation day.

Discussion:

  • 10-minute discussion after each paper presentation
  • Discuss in small groups of 2-6 students
  • Presenters prepare approximately 5 discussion questions
  • Each group submits a short discussion summary to Gradescope (one submission per group, mentioning the names)

Final Project: The final project is a major component of the course. Teams of approximately 3-5 students, three milestones (proposal, mid-project, final poster + report), poster session on Dec 7 and report due Dec 9. See the Final Project page for the full description, timeline, and report requirements.

Grading:

  • 50%: Final Project
  • 23%: Paper Reviews + Active Discussions
  • 25%: Class Presentation
    • 5%: Pre- and Post-Presentation Slide Submission
    • 20%: In-class presentation
  • 2%: Course Feedback

Piazza Rules:

  • We use Piazza for discussions on conceptual and technical questions among classmates. There is no anonymity. Please be respectful to your classmates.
  • Please post your questions, answers, and comments in dedicated folders. Each paper we read in the class has its own thread of discussions.
  • Please check Piazza for already posted questions before posting a new one. Unnecessarily clogging up Piazza makes the platform less usable for everybody.
  • Please use Piazza for all communications, private or public, as much as possible. Others will benefit from answers and discussions on public questions.
  • Piazza is moderated by the teaching staff.
